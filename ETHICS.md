# Ethics

AutoDub is a tool for generating an unofficial English dialogue track from captions you already have access to.

1. **Do not redistribute** episode files or AutoDub audio masters.
2. **Do not claim** tracks are official studio dubs.
3. **Voices:** this repo ships **house/stock voices only** in public examples. Instant Voice Clone (IVC) from third-party dubs is **personal/operator-only** — never commit celebrity clone IDs or reference audio. You supply any refs/API voices under your own responsibility and the provider’s terms.
4. **No DRM circumvention** in this project (no decryption, HDCP stripping, or stream ripping aids).
5. Label outputs clearly as unofficial AI English when you share demos of the *pipeline UI*, not the underlying show.
